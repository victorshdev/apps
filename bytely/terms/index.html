2. Description of Service

Bytely is a mobile app for cleaning up your photo library. You swipe through your photos to decide what to keep and what to delete, and the app finds duplicates and near-identical shots for you. All processing happens locally on your device.

The free tier allows deleting up to 1 GB per day. Bytely Pro removes this daily limit.

3. Deleting Photos Is Your Decision

Bytely helps you decide which photos to delete. You make those decisions; the app carries them out through iOS, which asks for your confirmation before anything is removed.

Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app and remain recoverable for 30 days. After that period, or if you empty that album yourself, deletion is permanent and cannot be undone by us or by Apple.

If iCloud Photos is enabled, deletion applies to your iCloud library and therefore to your other devices. Please keep your own backups.

You are responsible for the photos you choose to delete. We are not liable for content removed as a result of your decisions.
