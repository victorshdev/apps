Clean up your photo library by swiping

Bytely shows your photos one at a time, whole and full screen. Swipe left to delete, right to keep, up to favorite, down to decide later. It finds duplicates and near-identical shots for you, and nothing is deleted until you confirm.

Frequently asked

I deleted photos but my iPhone storage did not change

Two reasons, and both are how iOS works. First, deleted photos sit in Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days. Open Photos → Recently Deleted → Delete All to get the space back right away.

Second, if Optimize iPhone Storage is on in Settings → Photos, your originals live in iCloud and your iPhone only keeps compressed copies. Deleting then frees space in iCloud rather than on the device.

Can I undo a swipe?

Yes. Every decision can be undone, and nothing leaves your library until you confirm the delete queue.

How do I restore a deleted photo?

Photos app → Albums → Recently Deleted. Photos stay recoverable there for 30 days.

What does Bytely Pro include?

The free version deletes up to 1 GB per day. Pro removes that daily limit. Manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your iPhone, including duplicate detection. There are no accounts, no servers, and no analytics.
