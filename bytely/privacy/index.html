Bytely
Swipe to clean up photos

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Bytely helps you clean up your photo library, and it does that entirely on your iPhone. We have no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. Your photos, and every decision you make about them, never leave your device.

1. Your Photos Stay on Your Device

Bytely asks for access to your photo library so it can show your photos, group duplicates and similar shots, and delete the ones you swipe away.

  • Photos are read on your device and shown to you. They are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Duplicate and similarity detection runs on your iPhone using Apple's on-device Vision framework. No image and no image fingerprint ever leaves the device.
  • Deletion is performed by iOS itself: the app asks the system to delete the photos you selected, and iOS shows you its own confirmation.
  • Bytely never modifies, copies, or exports the contents of your photos.

2. What Bytely Stores on Your iPhone

To do its job, the app keeps some data in its own storage on your device:

  • Your decisions — kept, deleted, favorited, postponed — and the delete queue
  • Small thumbnails and image fingerprints used to find duplicates, cached so repeat scans are fast
  • File sizes of your photos, so the app can show how much space you free
  • Your settings: pinned albums, sorting order, appearance, haptics

All of it lives on your iPhone and is removed when you delete the app. None of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else.

3. Purchases and Subscriptions

Payment is handled by Apple. We never see or receive your payment details.

To know whether your Bytely Pro purchase is active, we use RevenueCat, a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user ID generated by the app, the product you purchased, and the receipt issued by Apple. It does not receive your name, your email, your Apple Account, or anything about your photos.

4. No Analytics, No Advertising, No Tracking

Bytely contains no analytics SDK, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. We do not build a profile of you, and there is nothing here to opt out of.

5. Children

Bytely is rated 4+ and collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

6. Your Choices

  • Revoke access to photos at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Bytely.
  • Remove everything the app stored by deleting the app. Your photos are not affected.
  • Ask us to delete purchase records held by RevenueCat by emailing us at the address below.

7. Data Retention

Data stored by the app stays on your device until you delete the app. Purchase records held by RevenueCat are retained for as long as needed to honor your subscription and to meet accounting obligations.

8. International Processing

RevenueCat may process purchase records on servers located in the United States.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
